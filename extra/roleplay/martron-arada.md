# Martron Arada — The Stranded Prince

A roleplay guide for an SSF run in Path of Exile, played with a Hardcore mindset. It covers the young Martron, before the Black Cube, before Khort, before the Empire.

---

## Premise

Martron is 15 when the Great Collapse comes. King Aran sends him and General Buran through a vortex to a parallel universe. In the canonical branch they wake up near Ranehald. **In this branch the vortex tears apart.** Martron wakes up alone and half-drowned on the Twilight Strand of Wraeclast, with no mentor, no escort and no knowledge of where he is.

The Omniverse contains every alternate reality, so this branch sits beside the canon without contradicting it.

## Two readings of the crossing

Both readings are valid, and neither is the true one. Choose per run, or give each life its own.

**A. The Stolen Life.** The spell carried only his soul. He wakes up in the body of a Wraeclast exile and takes over that person's life.
- He doesn't look like himself. Any class is possible, including the female ones.
- The body of light stayed behind. Only the soul crossed, and the soul is where the Darkness Itself lives. He arrives with the darkness intact and the light gone, which explains why he's far weaker than his lore suggests.
- He keeps the Lucent Form, because it's knowledge, not muscle. He doesn't keep the Aryzelian body, so his skill outruns what the body can do. Levelling is his soul reshaping the host towards what he used to be.
- Hooks: whose life did he take? Does anyone in Wraeclast recognise the face? Sin knows.
- Class: Duelist still works. **Scion** stands out: in PoE canon she's a young noblewoman, so one exiled royal ends up inside another. The Ascendant also borrows from every other class, which suits a soul remaking a body.

**B. The Prince in Exile.** He crossed whole: body, armour and all.
- He looks like a prince of Aryzel. His gear is what he carried out of the collapse.
- He keeps the body of light and the Aryzelian physicality. The rest of this guide assumes this reading unless it says otherwise.

## Who he is at 15

- **Body of pure light, soul harbouring the Darkness Itself.** He doesn't understand either one yet.
- **Trained by the best, tested by no one.** Aryzel's greatest warriors taught him the Lucent Form. They are veterans who fought demons alongside angels, so the form is war-proven, not ornamental. But Martron himself was a protected prince and has never had to use it for real, alone.
- **Aryzelian by nature.** Faster and stronger than any human has a right to be. This is his people's baseline, not magic.
- **No magic yet.** The light is what his body is made of, not something he casts. The darkness will be his first real magic.
- **Carries grief and duty.** His father, his kingdom and his only guardian are all lost. He wants protection and mentorship (see *Motivations to serve Khort*), and in Wraeclast whoever offers them first will shape him.
- **Mortal in body, not in soul.** The Black Cube hasn't found him yet, so nothing can resurrect him. But when his body dies, his soul doesn't leave (see *Death, rebirth and the Echo*).

## How he fights

The **Lucent Form** is all he has: a thinking discipline that happens to involve a blade.

- **Opening Read → Second Cut.** Against anything unknown, the first exchange is a question, not an attack. He probes, watches the response, then commits.
- **Trash isn't worth a read.** Against the clearly outmatched he doesn't probe, and he doesn't wade in either. He ends it from where he stands, as economically as possible. The blade up close is for opponents who've earned it. Hardcore mindset: don't walk into packs that aren't worth it.
- **Stillpoint.** He stays still between exchanges instead of circling, and makes the enemy commit first.
- **What's missing in this branch.**
  - *Buran's six years*, which turned trained skill into lived experience.
  - *Brandveg*, the free-hand technique of Ranehald.
  - *Khort's draughts.*
  
  Wraeclast has to teach him the first. He never gets the second or third, although he does find Wraeclast's own draughts (flasks) and doesn't fully trust them.
- **His flaw is inexperience plus confidence.** He was trained to fight as part of Aryzel's army, beside angels, against demons he had studied. In Wraeclast nobody guards his flank, and nothing behaves the way his books said it would. Misjudging which enemies are "worth reading" is how he gets hurt.

**For the Hardcore mindset, the Opening Read *is* the survival rule.** Against a new monster, boss or league mechanic, spend the first exchange dodging and watching before committing.

## Themes

- **Destiny vs. free will.** Is the darkness his fate or his choice?
- **Identity & transformation.** A prince of light slowly becoming the Son of Darkness.
- **Legacy.** The seeds of the Black Emperor's philosophy are planted here.

## Sin: the voice of the Darkness

In this story, **Sin is a representative of the Darkness Itself**, speaking to the part of Martron's soul that already belongs to it.

- **The mirror.** In PoE canon, Sin and Innocence (a god of light) are brothers. In the Omniverse, the Darkness Itself and the True Light are brothers. Martron is both in one person: a body of light and a soul of darkness.
- **Not a tempter, a teacher.** Sin plays the Darkness's three roles:
  - *Catalyst of Change*: breaks down what Martron was so he can become more.
  - *Guardian of Secrets*: reveals truths about Aryzel, the demons and Martron's birth, but only a little at a time.
  - *Tester of Will*: puts Martron in situations where he has to choose.
- **Sin never forces.** The darkness offers, and Martron chooses. This becomes the origin of the Emperor's later conviction that free will must never be taken away: *"What value is there in liberation if it means to deny what makes a being free in the first place?"*
- **Mentor with a hidden agenda.** In this branch Sin fills the role Khort plays in the canon: the teacher whose true aims Martron only understands later.
- **Outside time.** The Darkness exists outside space and time, so Sin remembers every one of Martron's lives and how each one ended (see *Death, rebirth and the Echo*).

## Death, rebirth and the Echo

### The first four lives

- **His soul reincarnates.** When his body dies, the soul doesn't pass on. The Darkness Itself lives in it and won't let go. The soul is flung back through the crossing, and he wakes again on the Twilight Strand, half-drowned, with nothing. Every life begins on the *Twilight* Strand. Much later, a cult will call him Sha'ol, God of Twilight.
- **Each death cost a body.** That body and all the strength it had built were gone for good.
- **The soul remembers; the body doesn't.** He keeps the Lucent Form and the memory of how he died. What you learned as a player, he learned as a soul.
- **Per reading.**
  - *A. The Stolen Life:* every rebirth steals another exile's body, so every death costs someone else their life too.
  - *B. The Prince in Exile:* the soul rebuilds the body of light on the strand, weak again until it regrows.
- **Sin remembers every life** and can open each new one with how the last one ended.

### The Echo

After the fourth death, the soul did **not** return to the Strand. It followed its fallen body into a softer echo of Wraeclast. *(The run continues in softcore SSF with the character that died to Innocence.)*

- **In the Echo, death no longer ends a life.** The soul drags the body back every time. But each fall costs part of what the body has learned. *(Later in the game, softcore deaths cost experience.)*
- **The caches stay behind.** What he hid in the harder Wraeclast is out of reach now. *(The Hardcore stash stays in the Hardcore league.)*
- **Other fallen shells walk here too:** the bodies of earlier lives, abandoned but not gone. *(Dead Hardcore characters that weren't deleted.)*
- **Why this time?** Maybe it was Sin's doing. Maybe it was the first choice Martron made against the pull of the Strand. He refused to start over. *(See Open threads.)*

### The Hardcore mindset

Every fall counts, even when it no longer ends a life:
- Log it, name the cause, and fix it before continuing.
- Two falls to the same thing means changing something (gear, level or approach) before a third try.
- Resistances capped, flasks modded, at or above the area level for bosses, and the Opening Read against anything new.

## Gameplay constraints

- **Class: Duelist.** It's male and the sword class (Strength/Dexterity). The Lucent Form is a swordsman's discipline, and one-hand swords need the Dexterity a Templar lacks.
- **Ascendancy: Gladiator.** In 3.29 it's built around **block and bleed**, the two engines of this Martron: the shield that guards his flank, and the wound the Second Cut deepens. It's tanky and Hardcore-friendly, and it's the dueling ascendancy for a fighter trained in a dueling tradition. (Champion, with impale and banners, was the original pick before the build turned to bleed.)
- **Weapon: one-hand sword + shield.** In Aryzel, the army and the angels guarded his flank. Alone in Wraeclast, the shield does.
- **Damage:** physical, with **bleeding** as the main engine, delivered through the blade. Think in two roles rather than fixed skills:
  - *Clearing* (trash isn't worth a read): something that ends packs quickly, ideally from range.
  - *Single target* (the worthy opponent): a blade skill that rewards reading first, then committing.
  - Darkness is added by phase. Keep one main damage type; PoE scales split damage badly.
- **Starting setup (early game only).** Splitting Steel for clearing, Double Strike + Ruthless for single target, War Banner as the banner of Aryzel, and Dash for movement. These are a starting point, not the build. Expect to replace them as the character and the arc develop.
- **Defences: life + block** (the Gladiator's core, and what powers retaliation skills such as Eviscerate), layered with **Armour/Evasion + spell suppression**, which is Duelist's natural part of the tree.
- **Character-style rules.** Restrictions cost style or damage, never defence.
- Check the specific skills in Path of Building against the current patch before committing.

**Probably leave out:**
- The small white wings. He fought beside angels; he isn't one.
- The antlers, the green skull armour, the rose weapon effect and the icy effects.
- The Vaal feather pieces, except for Reading A's phase 1.

**Worth buying:**
- *Valkyrie Smite Effect*: an angelic warrior effect on his main skill, for phases 1–2.
- The *Twilight* items (character effect, Sunrise weapon effect): day and night cycling, for phase 3. In the lorebook he's worshipped as Sha'ol, God of Twilight.
- A **black cape**: the Emperor's signature. The owned cloak is red.

**Hardcore mindset:** busy character and skill effects can hide ground effects and boss telegraphs. Test every effect in a map before committing to it.

- **The Black Cube.** Does it find him in this branch too, and does it find him *because* he listened to Sin?
- **Sin's agenda.** What does the Darkness Itself want from this particular Martron?
- **The bond.** Is the rebirth the Darkness Itself refusing to let go, and is the Black Cube later just a vessel for that same bond?
- **The Echo.** Why did the soul follow its body this time instead of returning to the Strand: Sin's doing, or Martron's first refusal?
- **The canon branch.** Do the Wraeclast Martron and the Ranehald Martron ever become aware of each other?
